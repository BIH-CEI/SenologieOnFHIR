This profile represents a surgical intervention performed on the breast or axilla as part of the BIH Senologie care specification — covering all operative procedures including primary tumor resection, sentinel lymph node biopsy, axillary dissection, reconstructive surgery, and revision procedures.

The profile constrains the FHIR `Procedure` resource by extending the German MII oncology parent profile `MII_PR_Onko_Mamma_Operation`, which in turn inherits from `MII_PR_Onko_Operation`. That parent chain already establishes oBDS-compliant structures for procedure coding (OPS and SNOMED CT slices), operative intention (`extension:Intention`), preoperative marking (`usedCode:PraeoperativeMarkierung`), and intraoperative imaging (`usedCode:IntraoperativesImaging`). This profile adds breast-center-specific constraints for operative category, post-operative orders, and performer roles without duplicating or overriding the inherited must-support obligations.

Key must-support elements specific to this profile are: `category`, bound to the `vs-senologie-operation-art` ValueSet and carrying the operation type, laterality, and axillary involvement; `code`, coded via the inherited OPS and SCT slices; `performedDateTime`, the date of the procedure; `subject`, restricted to `Patient` references; `complication`, using the inherited `compl_obds` and `compl_icd10` slices for oBDS and ICD-10-coded complications; `outcome`, capturing the operative result with particular relevance for revision procedures; `followUp`, sliced into five post-operative order categories (drainage, wound dressing, antibiotic therapy, mobilization, and laboratory follow-up), each fixed to a SNOMED CT concept; and `performer`, sliced into primary surgeon (`erstoperateur`, DKG field G08) and surgical assistant (`zweitoperateur`, G09).

Within the breast cancer care pathway this profile is instantiated at the time of surgical treatment, after histopathological confirmation of diagnosis. It serves as the primary procedure record linking the `senologie-diagnose-maligne` condition to downstream resources such as post-operative receptor-status observations, adjuvant therapy planning, and follow-up assessments. Implementers should populate both the `category` ValueSet binding and the inherited `code` OPS/SCT slices to satisfy cancer-registry reporting requirements.
