This profile represents the anatomical location of a breast tumor, capturing the laterality, quadrant, clock-face position, and distance from the nipple required for breast cancer staging and surgical planning.

The profile constrains the FHIR `BodyStructure` resource — specifically the MII Imaging `Koerperstruktur` base profile — because `BodyStructure` is the designated FHIR resource for describing a named, pre-coordinated or post-coordinated body site associated with a patient, and its built-in `location` and `locationQualifier` elements map directly to the multi-axis localization scheme used in clinical breast oncology.

Three must-support elements are defined. `location` carries the SNOMED CT concept for breast and must be present. `locationQualifier` is sliced into three independent qualifiers — `seitenlokalisation` (laterality: left, right, or bilateral), `quadrant` (e.g., upper-outer, lower-inner), and `uhrzeitposition` (clock-face position, bound to a required value set using the 12-hour scheme). `description` is a free-text must-support field intended for details that cannot be encoded, most commonly the measured distance from the nipple in millimeters. The `patient` element is restricted to a plain `Patient` reference.

In the breast cancer care pathway, this profile is referenced from diagnostic imaging reports and surgical or pathology findings to anchor every tumor-bearing resource to a precise anatomical site. It enables consistent spatial reasoning across imaging, biopsy, and therapy planning, and supports the traceability requirements of the BIH Senologie logical model.
