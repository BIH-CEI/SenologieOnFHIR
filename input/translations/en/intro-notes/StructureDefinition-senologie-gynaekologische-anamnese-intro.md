This profile represents a structured gynecological history collected as part of the breast cancer intake assessment, covering reproductive milestones and hormonal exposures known to influence breast cancer risk and treatment decisions.

The profile constrains the FHIR `Observation` resource, using its `component` pattern to group multiple related data points — menarche age, menopause status, number of pregnancies, and hormone replacement therapy (HRT) — into a single, cohesive clinical observation rather than scattering them across discrete resources. The panel code is LOINC `89221-6` (Gynecology History and physical note), providing an internationally recognized anchor for the observation as a whole.

All four components are must-support: `menarche` (LOINC `42798-9`, age in years as a UCUM `Quantity`), `menopause` (LOINC `42802-9`, accepting either a `Quantity` for age at menopause or a `CodeableConcept` for pre-/postmenopausal status), `schwangerschaften` (LOINC `11996-6`, gravida count as a `Quantity`), and `hormonersatztherapie` (SNOMED CT `266717002`, accepting a `CodeableConcept` or `boolean`). The status is fixed to `#final`; `subject` is restricted to `Patient`; and `effectiveDateTime` carries the date the history was recorded. Component slicing is open, allowing implementers to add site-specific extensions without breaking conformance.

In the breast cancer care pathway this observation sits at the point of initial workup and multidisciplinary tumor board preparation. Its data feeds risk stratification, eligibility assessment for hormone-sensitive therapies, and longitudinal documentation in the electronic health record. Systems implementing the Senologie IG should populate this profile during or immediately after the initial oncology consultation.
