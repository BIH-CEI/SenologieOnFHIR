This profile represents the umbrella documentation record for a completed systemic therapy course in breast cancer care, capturing the overarching treatment episode rather than individual drug administrations.

The profile constrains the `Procedure` resource — specifically the MII Oncology Systemic Therapy parent profile (`MII_PR_Onko_Systemische_Therapie`) — because systemic therapy is an active clinical intervention with a defined start, end, intent, and outcome, all of which map naturally to `Procedure` semantics. The `category` element is fixed to SNOMED CT `18629005` (Administration of medication) to satisfy the MII oncology validation rule `proc-mii-1` when OPS codes are present.

Key must-support elements are `status` (always `#completed`, reflecting that only concluded therapy episodes are documented at this level), `code` (the therapy type), `subject`, `outcome` (replacing a former extension — records the reason the therapy ended, e.g. completed, discontinued), and `reasonReference` (the linked breast cancer diagnosis). Two breast-cancer-specific extensions are added: `EX_Senologie_TherapyLine` for the sequential therapy line number and `EX_Senologie_FirstLineTherapy` to flag first-line treatment at metastatic onset (corresponding to OncoBox field KB-8). The `performed[x]` period (start/end dates), therapy intention, and protocol codes (`usedCode`) are inherited from the MII parent without further constraint.

Within the breast cancer care pathway, this profile sits at the episode level of systemic treatment — after staging and receptor profiling, and potentially referenced by cycle-level or agent-level `Procedure` resources via `partOf`. Implementers should populate `reasonReference` to the staging diagnosis and use `outcome` rather than any legacy status extension to record how the therapy concluded.
