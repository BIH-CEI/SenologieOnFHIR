This profile represents a single structured finding from a breast imaging examination, capturing standardized assessment categories such as BI-RADS classification, ACR breast density, mass/lesion characteristics, microcalcifications, and lymph node status.

The profile constrains the base FHIR `Observation` resource because breast imaging findings are discrete, coded clinical observations tied to a specific patient, laterality, and point in time — the natural fit for the Observation pattern. `status` is fixed to `#final`, ensuring that only completed, verified findings are exchanged. `subject`, `effectiveDateTime`, and `bodySite` are Must Support to anchor every finding to its patient, acquisition date, and lateralized anatomical region (left breast, right breast, or bilateral).

`code.coding` is sliced into ten named slices covering the five core finding types, each represented by both a primary terminology (LOINC or SNOMED CT) and a secondary radiology vocabulary (RadLex): `biRadsLoinc`/`biRadsSnomed`, `acrDensityLoinc`/`acrDensityRadlex`, `herdbefundSnomed`/`herdbefundRadlex`, `mikrokalkSnomed`/`mikrokalkRadlex`, and `lymphknotenSnomed`/`lymphknotenRadlex`. All slices are optional (0..1) and the slicing is open, allowing additional codes. `value[x]` is constrained to `CodeableConcept`, `Quantity`, or `string` to accommodate the varying result types across these finding categories; a warning-level invariant (`biRads-value`) enforces that BI-RADS results carry a coded value.

Within the breast cancer care pathway this profile sits at the imaging-assessment stage, upstream of histopathology and staging. Instances are surfaced through `DiagnosticReport.result` references from the companion `senologie-bildgebung-diagnosticreport` profile, which groups one or more of these observations into a cohesive imaging report. Implementers should populate at least the LOINC or SNOMED slice for each finding type to maximize interoperability across radiology information systems and oncology platforms.
