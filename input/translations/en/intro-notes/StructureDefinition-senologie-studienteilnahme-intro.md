This profile records a breast cancer patient's enrollment in a clinical trial, capturing her participation status, the associated study, the assigned treatment arm, and the date of informed consent.

The profile constrains the base FHIR `ResearchSubject` resource, which is the standard mechanism for linking a patient to a `ResearchStudy` and tracking her progress through the trial lifecycle. `ResearchSubject` carries a built-in status vocabulary (candidate, screening, eligible, on-study, withdrawn, off-study, and related codes) that directly aligns with the clinical workflow used in breast cancer trial management, making it the appropriate base without requiring a procedural workaround on another resource type.

The following elements are marked Must Support: `status` (participation status, drawn from the FHIR `ResearchSubject.status` value set), `study` (reference to the corresponding `ResearchStudy`), `individual` (reference to the `Patient`), `period` (participation interval), and `assignedArm` (the trial arm to which the patient is allocated). Four senology-specific extensions supplement the base resource where no native FHIR equivalent exists: `Screeningstatus` (a coded assessment of screening outcome, bound to an example value set backed by SNOMED CT), `Studienscreening` (a boolean indicating whether an active eligibility screening was performed, independent of its result), `Studienkontakt` (a free-text contact person for the trial), and `Aufklaerungsdatum` (the date on which the patient was informed and consented).

Within the breast cancer care pathway this profile sits at the intersection of diagnostics and treatment planning. It is typically populated after a tumor board decision has identified a potentially eligible patient and before or alongside the initiation of study-specific interventions. Implementers sourcing data from OncoBox 2.0 should map the K02 study-name field to `study.display` or a linked `ResearchStudy.title`, and the K03 screening flag to the `Studienscreening` extension boolean.
