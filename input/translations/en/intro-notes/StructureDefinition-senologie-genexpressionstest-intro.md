This profile represents the result of a genomic expression test used to stratify recurrence risk in hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative early breast cancer, covering validated assays including Oncotype DX, MammaPrint, Prosigna, and EndoPredict.

The profile constrains the base FHIR `RiskAssessment` resource because genomic expression tests produce a risk prediction — a qualitative risk class and an optional distant-recurrence probability — rather than a raw laboratory measurement, making `RiskAssessment` a more semantically precise fit than `Observation`. The numeric score itself is captured in a companion `Senologie_Genexpressions_Score` observation and referenced via `RiskAssessment.basis`, keeping the scalar result separate from its clinical interpretation.

Key must-support elements include `method` (required, bound to the `VS_Senologie_Genexpressionstest` value set to identify which assay was performed), `prediction.qualitativeRisk` (required, bound to `VS_Senologie_Risikoklasse` encoding low/intermediate/high risk according to each assay's validated cut-offs), `prediction.probabilityDecimal` (the 10-year distant-recurrence risk as a decimal), and `prediction.whenRange` (the prediction horizon, typically 10 years). The `condition` element links the risk assessment back to the primary breast cancer diagnosis, and `performer` records the responsible laboratory or clinician.

Within the breast cancer care pathway, this profile is populated after primary surgical and pathological workup when a genomic assay is ordered to inform adjuvant chemotherapy decisions. It is part of the `Genetik` (Genetics) module of the Senologie logical model and is typically exchanged alongside receptor-status and staging resources to support multidisciplinary tumor board documentation.
