This profile represents a PD-L1 immunohistochemistry (IHC) result in the context of breast cancer pathology, capturing the scoring data required to determine eligibility for immune checkpoint inhibitor therapy.

The profile derives from the MII MTB Immunohistochemistry PD-L1 profile (`mii-pr-mtb-immunohistochemistry-pdl1`), which itself constrains the FHIR `Observation` resource. Reusing the MII MTB parent ensures alignment with the German Medical Informatics Initiative's molecular tumor board data model while adding breast-cancer-specific constraints and guidance.

Key must-support elements are `status`, `code`, `subject`, `effectiveDateTime`, `method`, and the `component[cps-score]` slice. The `method` element is particularly important because the antibody clone used (e.g., SP142, 22C3, SP263) determines which therapeutic agent's companion diagnostic is satisfied. The Combined Positive Score (CPS) component is the primary scoring axis for breast cancer: a CPS ≥ 10 is the decision threshold for pembrolizumab in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) per KEYNOTE-522. The Immune Cell Score (ICS) component is additionally marked to support atezolizumab eligibility assessment per IMpassion130 (IC ≥ 1%).

Within the breast cancer care pathway, this profile is populated at the pathology reporting step following core-needle biopsy or surgical resection and feeds into the multidisciplinary tumor board decision for systemic therapy planning. Implementers should populate `specimen` to link the observation to the tissue source, and must record `effectiveDateTime` to support longitudinal comparisons when re-biopsy is performed after neoadjuvant treatment.
