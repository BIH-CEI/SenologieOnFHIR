This profile records the family history of breast and ovarian cancer for a breast cancer patient, capturing the degree of familial risk that informs hereditary risk assessment and genetic counseling decisions.

The profile constrains the core FHIR `FamilyMemberHistory` resource, which is the appropriate base for structured per-relative disease history. Using `FamilyMemberHistory` rather than a flat Observation allows each affected relative to be represented as a discrete entry with its own relationship type, age, and condition onset — reflecting the multi-relative nature of hereditary cancer risk evaluation.

Key must-support elements include `status`, `patient` (restricted to `Patient`), `relationship` (the degree of kinship, e.g. mother, sister, aunt, grandmother), `age[x]` (the relative's age), and `condition`. The `condition` element is sliced into two named slices: `mammakarzinom` (SNOMED CT 254837009 — malignant neoplasm of breast) and `ovarialkarzinom` (SNOMED CT 363443007 — malignant tumor of ovary), each carrying an `onset[x]` for the age of disease onset in that relative. The slicing is open, permitting additional conditions to be recorded. A free-text `note` field accommodates further familial burden that cannot be captured in the structured slices.

Within the breast cancer care pathway, this profile is completed during the initial oncological workup and feeds directly into hereditary breast and ovarian cancer (HBOC) risk stratification. It supports the assessment criteria for referral to genetic counseling (e.g., fulfillment of S3-Leitlinie or BRCA testing criteria) and is mapped to the `Familienanamnese` element of the BIH Senologie logical model.
