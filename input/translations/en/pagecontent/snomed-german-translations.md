### SNOMED CT German Translations

This page documents the status of German SNOMED CT translations for all codes used in the Senology Core Dataset (Kerndatensatz Senologie).

#### Summary

- **57 SNOMED CT codes** are used in the module
- **40 codes** (70%) have an official German translation (BfArM German Edition)
- **17 codes** (30%) require submission to the SNOMED CT German Translation Group (BfArM)

#### Submission Notes

- **P1 codes** (10 codes): Diagnoses and B3 lesions, clinically relevant for cancer registry reporting
- **P2 codes** (7 codes): Clinical findings and administrative codes
- Submission via the [BfArM SNOMED CT Translation Request Portal](https://www.bfarm.de/DE/Kodiersysteme/Terminologien/SNOMED-CT/_node.html)
- All codes were validated against SNOMED CT International Edition 2025-12-01 and the German Edition
