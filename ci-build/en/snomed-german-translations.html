This page documents the status of German SNOMED CT translations for all codes used in the Senology Core Dataset (Kerndatensatz Senologie).

Summary

  • 57 SNOMED CT codes are used in the module
  • 40 codes (70%) have an official German translation (BfArM German Edition)
  • 17 codes (30%) require submission to the SNOMED CT German Translation Group (BfArM)
