Therapy

​Note on medication coding: In SDC questionnaires, only one coding can be stored per form field. Medication is therefore coded primarily in SNOMED CT, as SNOMED CT offers the broadest coverage as a reference terminology. For cancer registry reporting (oBDS), ATC is required — the translation is performed via the ConceptMap SNOMED CT → ATC. This avoids double coding in the form and the reporting data are derived automatically.

CS Senologie Diagnosesicherung

12 codes per oBDS key (1–9 with subclasses). These are oBDS-specific and have no direct SNOMED equivalent, as they reflect the German cancer registry reporting process.
