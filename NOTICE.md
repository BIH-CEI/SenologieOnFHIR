## National profiles and reporting specifications

The guide builds on profiles of the Medizininformatik-Initiative (MII) core
dataset and of ISiK (gematik), and it maps to the reporting specifications oBDS,
OncoBox Brust (Deutsche Krebsgesellschaft / OnkoZert), IQTIG QS 18/1 and the
German implant registry (IRegG). These remain under the terms of their
respective publishers. The schema files under `input/data/` are copies provided
for testing the mappings.
